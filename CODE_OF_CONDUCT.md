# Community guidelines

Changefence is built for practical collaboration. We want contributors and users to be able to disagree, investigate failures, and improve the project without making participation hostile.

## Expected

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique code, behavior, policy, and evidence—not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for reproducible evidence.
- Make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that are relevant to a proposal or review.
- Respect privacy and use private reporting for security-sensitive information.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.
- Using project spaces to promote scams, malware, or deceptive claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these guidelines, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on keeping the community safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. For vulnerabilities or reports containing sensitive repository details, use [GitHub Security Advisories](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.
